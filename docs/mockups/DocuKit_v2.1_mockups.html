DocuKit v2.1: refreshed UI, Home tour and ad placements

Visual reference for UI_REFRESH.md, TOUR.md and ADS.md. The striped boxes are ad slots; their content comes from AdMob. Ad slots are dashed and labelled "Ad" so they never look like tools. With no network or no ad, every slot disappears.

Where ads never appear

  • Tool screens, because the "Compress image" button sits there and ads beside it cause accidental taps, which AdMob penalises.
  • Processing, Error, the page organizer, dialogs, onboarding, the tour and Privacy & about.
  • Anywhere, after the user buys Remove ads, or when there is no network or no ad to show.
